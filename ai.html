How we use AI

A short account of what a model sees, who hosts it, and what StaffMatch keeps. Last updated September 6, 2026.

What a model does — and what it does not

StaffMatch uses a language model for one simple task: reading your uploaded documents and pulling out key information like skills, roles, and requirements. The actual matching of people to projects happens without AI.

  • Extract. When you upload a resume, proposal, or RFP, we read the text and ask a language model to identify important details — things like names, skills, job titles, and project requirements. This organized information is what powers the rest of StaffMatch.
  • Match. Finding the right people for projects works by comparing those organized details — no AI involved. We look for keyword matches and overlapping skills using straightforward scoring.

StaffMatch helps you make better decisions faster, but you're always in control of your staffing and proposal choices.

The AI service we use

We send documents to a language model through OpenRouter, a service that connects to various AI providers.

More info

The specific model ID is configured on our server (not visible in this public site). Extract requests route through OpenRouter's API.

Training

StaffMatch does not train its own models on your documents.

OpenRouter is a routing layer that connects to various AI model providers. We don't control their account settings or training policies.

Here's what OpenRouter's public documentation says (as of this writing):

  • They don't store your prompts or responses unless you specifically opt in. They do keep basic request information like token counts and which model was used. See Data Collection.
  • They don't use your inputs or outputs to train OpenRouter's own models. See their Privacy Policy.
  • The companies that actually run the AI models have their own rules about data logging and training. Free models in particular may allow the provider to train on your requests. OpenRouter documents this on Provider Logging and on each model page.

If you need guaranteed no-training or zero-retention handling, you'll need to confirm that directly with OpenRouter and the specific AI provider. We can't make that promise here.

More info

OpenRouter functions as middleware between StaffMatch and upstream model hosts. Each host (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, or others) maintains separate logging, retention, and training policies. Free-tier OpenRouter routes may allow the upstream provider to use requests for training purposes. Consult OpenRouter's provider-specific documentation and the host's terms of service for binding commitments.

What StaffMatch keeps

  • We save the key fields pulled from your documents — things like names, skills, and requirements — in our database.
  • We don't keep the original PDF files you upload by default.
  • Any temporary files created during processing live on our server's disk, which is wiped clean whenever the server restarts or redeploys.

We do not sell your information. Account passwords go only to Supabase Auth — never by email and never through this site's marketing forms. See the Pilot Terms.

More info

Structured extracts are persisted in Postgres. Original uploaded files are not retained by default. Temporary JSON artifacts may be written to the app server's filesystem (Render ephemeral disk on the free plan; lost on restart, redeploy, or spin-down).
