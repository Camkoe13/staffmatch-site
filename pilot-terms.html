Pilot Terms

Short terms for the free 1-month StaffMatch pilot. Last updated September 5, 2026.

  1. What you get. For about one month, we help you try StaffMatch with a thin cut of your roster and a few real RFPs. No fee for this pilot period.
  2. What you provide. You share only data you are allowed to share (roster exports, sample RFPs). Do not send confidential client material you cannot disclose.
  3. Not a commitment to buy. The pilot is free and does not obligate you to a paid plan. We will not auto-charge you.
  4. Independence. StaffMatch is an independent product and is not affiliated with your employer or any other firm unless we say so in writing.
  5. No warranty. The pilot is provided as-is. Matching output is assistive; you remain responsible for staffing and proposal decisions.
  6. Privacy. We use your contact details only to run the pilot and follow up. We will not sell your info.
  7. Contact. Questions: staffmatch.support@gmail.com.
